What arrives

Open the box and start.

  1. Gems in the exact colours and sizes used on that cover
  2. Tools and gem trays for sorting and placing
  3. Glue, wax and a sealer
  4. A card of tips and tricks for bedazzling your book

Gem amounts are measured from books Harley has bedazzled herself, then weighed, so the count that arrives is the count the cover needs.
