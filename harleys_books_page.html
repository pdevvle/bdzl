How it works

Three steps to a shelf piece

Sort your gems

Tip each color into the trays. Everything arrives measured out in the right amount for the design — no guessing, no running short.

Follow the design

Use the reference photos as your guide. Pick up a row of stones with the multi-gem picker and lay them straight onto the cover.

Show it off

Stand it face-out on the shelf, gift it to the friend who won't stop talking about the series, or start the next one.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need to buy anything else?

No. Each kit comes with the curated gems plus a large and small sorting tray and a large and small multi-gem picker. If you'd like the book included in the order, that's an option too.

Will the kit fit my copy?

Kits are built for one specific edition — the one named in the listing — because gem counts and colors are measured for that exact cover. For any other edition, message first and a custom kit can be made.

What does "pre-order" mean?

New titles open as pre-orders while the gems are sourced and the kits are packed by hand. The listing states the current timing before you check out.

Have you done this before?

Harley's Books is the original creator of the Book Bedazzle Kit — the shop has been packing them since the trend started.

Where can I see finished books?

Tips, tricks and finished covers go up on Instagram @Harleysbooks and TikTok @harleysbookto.
